---
title: "Design is the goal handed to the Worker, not a review after the fact"
date: 2026-08-12
---

An implementing agent optimizes toward whatever goal it is handed; hand it a feature ticket and design
quality becomes whatever survives shipping. So the Guide hands the Worker a design/quality objective
with the feature as a constraint, and measures the returned design — direction and measurement, not
coercion. Good design is produced at construction time, not policed afterward.
