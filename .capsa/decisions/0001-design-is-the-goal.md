---
title: "Design is the goal handed to the Worker, not a review after the fact"
date: 2026-08-12
---

Context: an implementing agent optimizes toward whatever goal it is handed; hand it a feature ticket
and design quality becomes whatever survives shipping.

Decision: the Guide hands the Worker a design/quality objective with the feature as a constraint, and
measures the returned design — direction and measurement, not coercion.

Consequences: good design is produced at construction time, not policed afterward. Inherited from
Balash; the reason aims exists.
