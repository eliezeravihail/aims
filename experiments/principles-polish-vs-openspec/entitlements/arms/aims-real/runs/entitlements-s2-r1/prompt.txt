# Entitlements — stage 2

The authorization model gains three capabilities. They combine in a single decision.

1. **Explicit deny.** A grant may be an **allow** or an explicit **deny**. An explicit deny for `(A, R)`
   applied to any of U's roles **overrides** any allow — even an allow from a different role. (Deny wins.)

2. **Resource inheritance.** Resources form a hierarchy: a resource may belong to a **group** (e.g. `doc-42`
   is in folder `finance`, which is in `org-root`). A grant on a group applies to every resource beneath it,
   unless a more specific grant on a descendant says otherwise. A deny anywhere on the path still wins over
   an allow.

3. **Time-bounded grants.** A grant may carry a validity window `[from, to)`. Outside its window the grant
   does not apply. The decision is made relative to a supplied "now".

The stage-1 question and its answer for a simple role→permission grant are unchanged where these features
are absent.

Update the architecture to absorb these capabilities. Design only.
