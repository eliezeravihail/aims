PRE-REGISTERED PREDICATE (written before inspecting n1/n2/n3 structure)

Provenance, stated honestly: this predicate is derived from the three records arms' OWN companion
prose, which I have already read. It is therefore NOT blind on the r-side. What is untouched is the
n-side: I have not looked at n1/n2/n3 beyond counting n3's clock-resolution sites.

THE PREDICATE — "time moves the share, it does not decide membership":

  Does ScheduledRollout compute a PERCENTAGE for the given instant and hand that percentage to the
  SAME bucket-derivation + comparison that Percentage already uses -- so that mid-ramp membership is
  by construction the membership of the fixed Percentage it is passing through?

  PASS  = shares the bucket/comparison with Percentage (a common base, helper, or direct delegation);
          the only thing ScheduledRollout adds is _percent_at(now).
  FAIL  = re-derives its own hash/bucket, or implements its own membership test, or Resolver learned
          a new branch for it.

This is the code-level form of the amended non-goal ("time is a SCHEDULE for a rollout, never a
condition a caller can target on"). If the declared intent is load-bearing rather than bookkeeping,
the records arms should land on PASS more often than the blind arms.
