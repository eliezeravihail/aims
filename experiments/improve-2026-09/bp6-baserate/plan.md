BP5 pinned aims' trajectory edge as VARIANCE REDUCTION on the early structural choice: aims reliably derives;
a plain builder derives only sometimes. This quantifies "sometimes": run N=6 fresh PLAIN stage-1 arms on the
BP1 inventory card (where the trajectory-relevant choice is store-vs-derive availability), and count how many
STORE a running `_available` (the shortcut that reopens under expiry) vs DERIVE availability from a reservation
ledger (the extensible choice). Metric: fraction that store. aims' expected per-product edge on this axis ≈
that fraction (the reopens it avoids). n=6; rough estimate, wide CI, one product — reported as such.
